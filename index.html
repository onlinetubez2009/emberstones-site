How deep does it go?

Four biomes stacked one under the other. Each has its own enemies, its own tiles and its own bosses.

  1. Floor1

    Stone Dungeon

    Floors 1 to 20. Where every descent begins. Gnolls, traps and the first wardens of the deep.

  2. Floor21

    Catacombs

    Floors 21 to 40. Halls of the restless dead, lit by candles that never go out.

  3. Floor41

    Inferno

    Floors 41 to 60. Forges, lava and ash. The heat is only the first problem.

  4. Floor61

    Flooded Archives

    Floors 61 to 80. A drowned library where the water rises and the books bite back.
