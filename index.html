Labyrinth in Fragments

Descend through 80 procedurally generated floors across four biomes. Read the telegraphs, outsmart bosses that remember how you fought them, and see how deep you can go. Every run is different.

How deep does it go?

Four biomes stacked one under the other, a safe camp after each. Every biome has its own creatures, its own dangers and two bosses.

  1. Floor1

    Stone Dungeon

    Floors 1 to 20. Where every descent begins. Gnolls, traps and the first wardens of the deep.

    Bosses
    Gnoll Warlord, Chain Bailiff
    Camp
    Gravedigger's Rest
  2. Floor21

    Catacombs

    Floors 21 to 40. Halls of the restless dead, lit by candles that never go out.

    Bosses
    Ossuary Matron, the Pale Regent
    Camp
    The Last Vigil
  3. Floor41

    Inferno

    Floors 41 to 60. Forges, lava and ash. The heat is only the first problem.

    Bosses
    Forgemaster Ignarus, the Ashen Duchess
    Camp
    The Quenching Yard
  4. Floor61

    Flooded Archives

    Floors 61 to 80. A drowned library where the water rises and the books bite back.

    Bosses
    The Drowned Archivist, the Tide-Scribe
    Camp
    The Margin
  5. Floor81

    Crystal Mines Coming next

    Floors 81 to 100. Violet crystal grows through the timbers. The Labyrinth's mind lies in pieces here.
