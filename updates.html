Updates

What's new in the Labyrinth.

Every update since Halloween, newest first. The same list is behind the What's new button in the game.

  1. 26 Nov 2026

    The Flooded Archives

    Chapter 4 is out: floors 61 to 80, a drowned library where the water rises and falls.

    • Seven new creatures, from Drowned Scholars and Ink Slimes to Sirens, Eels and Lightning Jellies.
    • Shallow, ankle-deep and deep water. Currents carry you and your loot, and lightning spreads through the water.
    • Two new bosses: the Drowned Archivist and the Tide-Scribe.
    • Five new rooms, scrollcraft at the Scriptorium, the Archivist Set, and The Margin, the camp after floor 80.
  2. 25 Nov 2026

    Special rooms rebuilt

    Every special room has its own shape and new furniture.

    • Room windows and Orlo's shop open as one big window in the middle of the screen.
    • Your active effects sit in the hero window, under your health bar.
  3. 24 Nov 2026

    A new backpack

    One big leather-bound window with pages.

  4. 23 Nov 2026

    Game passes, talents and real flames

    The Royal Treasury, the Star Codex and flickering torches.

    • Starter Kit passes (Vanguard, Warden, Scout) and Merchant's Friend.
    • The talent screen is now the Star Codex: three paths, one point per level.
    • Torches, candles and braziers burn with real flickering flames.
    • Party play is out for now; co-op comes back later as game modes of its own.
  5. 22 Nov 2026

    New icons and a real Journal

    Every item icon redrawn as pixel art, and the Journal is a real book.

    • Every biome has its own stairs, and the last stairs of a biome lead down into the next one.
    • Click-to-walk goes round chests and barrels instead of opening them on the way.
    • Bosses wake as soon as you step off the stairs.
  6. 21 Nov 2026

    The Inferno and armour sets

    Chapter 3: floors 41 to 60, the Foundry Below, with lava.

    • Imps, Magma Slimes, Hellhounds, Fire Elementals, Demon Smiths and Obsidian Golems.
    • Two new bosses with their own entrances: Forgemaster Ignarus and the Ashen Duchess.
    • Five new rooms and the Quenching Yard, the camp after floor 60.
    • Four armour slots and a set for every biome, with 2 and 4 piece bonuses.
  7. 19 Nov 2026

    A new look

    Every model in the game was rebuilt: still blocky, far more detailed.

    • Weaknesses are spread over every damage type, so no single weapon wins every fight.
  8. 13 Nov 2026

    Room guides

    Walk into a special room and a card tells you what to do there.

  9. 12 Nov 2026

    The Catacombs

    Chapter 2: floors 21 to 40, the Ossuary.

    • Twelve new creatures, from Skeletons and Ghouls to Necromancers and Cursed Coffins.
    • Two new bosses: the Ossuary Matron and the Pale Regent.
    • Six new rooms and the Last Vigil, the camp after floor 40.
  10. 5 Nov 2026

    Biomes and bosses

    The dungeon splits into biomes, and bosses arrive.

    • The Gnoll Warlord and the Chain Bailiff. Each boss is met twice, and remembers how you fought it.
    • Fragments of the Labyrinth's Memory to collect, and Gravedigger's Rest after floor 20.
  11. 30 Oct 2026

    Halloween: the Pumpking Reaver

    A seasonal class with a scythe and a pumpkin for a head.

    • Journal 2.0: creature pages that grow as you meet them, and pages of an old tale to find.

Next down: the Crystal Mines.
