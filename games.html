How it plays

Turn-based tactics

Every move counts. Enemies act when you act, so think before you step.

Procedural floors

A new layout every run. No two descents are the same.

Telegraphed bosses

Attacks are shown on the floor before they land. Read the warnings, then outplay them.

Loot and crafting

Tiered locked chests, armour sets and recipes to discover.

Classes and kits

Pick how you start. Each class shapes how you play from floor one.

The Journal

Collect lore pages and fill in the bestiary as you explore.

Early access

Labyrinth in Fragments is in early access. New biomes, bosses and features land regularly. Report bugs and suggest ideas on our Discord.
